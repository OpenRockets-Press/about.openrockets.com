# What is OpenRockets Magazine?

OpenRockets Magazine is not only a magazine that OpenRockets uses to announce organization updates, it is also magazine software. It is provided at no charge to nonprofits and K12 schools, including private, county, catholic and public schools, that need their own digital publication. Any organization that signs up gets a fully set-up instance of the software. It requires no programming knowledge to run. The organization signs in through OpenRockets Accounts Fast Sign In, publishes under its own branding and theme, and receives a dedicated address on zeroprofit.org. Organizations are also welcome to connect their own domain instead of using the zeroprofit.org address.

To sign up for OpenRockets Magazine, anyone can do so through the OpenRockets Accounts system at myaccount.openrockets.com, regardless of whether their club already owns a magazine or is starting one for the first time.

OpenRockets Magazine was the first product of the team.
